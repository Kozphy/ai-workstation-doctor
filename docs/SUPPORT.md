# Support & commercial use

## Community (free)

This repository is the **Community** edition of AI Workstation Doctor:

- Local scan, port checker, Windows system/GPU facts, redacted Markdown reports
- MIT licensed — use it, fork it, share redacted reports when asking for help
- Issues and PRs welcome for collectors, checks, and docs

Nothing is sent off-device. Scans stay on your machine.
